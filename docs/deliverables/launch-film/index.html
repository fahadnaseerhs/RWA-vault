08Anchor

09Prove

12 hashes prove one action among 4,096.

Any user downloads a short proof and checks it offline. No trust in us required.

10Dispute

Challenge it, and the full verifier runs.

One contested envelope is checked on chain in full, paid for by the challenger. The expensive proof runs only where a fault is claimed.
